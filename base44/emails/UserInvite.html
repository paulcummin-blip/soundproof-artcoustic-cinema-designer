Your Project Tokens

For every £10,000 of qualifying Artcoustic loudspeakers ordered at trade prices, your dealer account earns 5 Project Tokens. Your turnover for 2026 qualifies for your Project Tokens already achieved and available.

Each Project Token allows you to create one new cinema project.

Additional Project Tokens can be purchased through Sound Proof when purchasing becomes available.

Your earned and purchased Project Tokens remain in your account until used.

Explore up to five designs per project

Each cinema project includes up to five design variations, allowing you to explore different speaker layouts, screen sizes, seating arrangements and other design choices for the same client.

A fair usage policy applies. Design variations are intended for alternative approaches to the same physical cinema, not unrelated projects.
